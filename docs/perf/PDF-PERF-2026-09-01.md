## Defect found and fixed

**[HIGH][PERF/RELY] Off-screen page canvases were never released on long documents.**

`shouldReleaseDistantPages()` only returned `true` when zoom > 1.5x, or for an
Archive.org scan on a <4GB device. For a normal batch-notes PDF at 1x on any phone
reporting >=4GB (or no `deviceMemory` at all — the common Android case, which falls
back to 4GB) it returned `false`, so every page scrolled past kept its bitmap alive.
Measured: **40 of 40 canvases still mounted** after one pass through a 40-page PDF.
A 300-page batch note at 3x DPR is ~100MB+ of bitmap under that rule — the classic
low-RAM WebView OOM.

Fix (`src/lib/pdfCanvasBudget.ts`, `src/components/video/FastPdfReader.tsx`):
release distant pages whenever the document exceeds `LONG_DOCUMENT_PAGES` (20),
in addition to the existing zoom and low-memory rules. The 1200px
`IntersectionObserver` margin in `usePageSlotVisibility` keeps neighbours mounted,
so scrolling stays flicker-free — confirmed by the unchanged long-frame ratio and
the faster cold/warm open above.

Regression guard: `src/test/pdfCanvasRelease.test.ts` (4 assertions).
